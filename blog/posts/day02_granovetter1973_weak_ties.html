Abstract · 摘要
Analysis of social networks is suggested as a tool for linking micro and macro levels of sociological theory. The procedure is illustrated by elaboration of the macro implications of one aspect of small-scale interaction: the strength of dyadic ties. It is argued that the degree of overlap of two individuals' friendship networks varies directly with the strength of their tie to one another. The impact of this principle on diffusion of influence and information, mobility opportunity, and community organization is explored. Stress is laid on the cohesive power of weak ties. Most network models deal, implicitly, with strong ties, thus confining their applicability to small, well-defined groups. Emphasis on weak ties lends itself to discussion of relations between groups and to analysis of segments of social structure not easily defined in terms of primary groups.
本文提出，社会网络分析可以作为连接社会学理论微观层次与宏观层次的一种工具。文章通过详细阐述小规模互动的某一方面——dyadic ties（二元关系）的强度——所具有的宏观意涵，来例示这一研究程序。本文的核心论点是：两个人各自友谊网络的重叠程度，与他们彼此之间关系（tie，关系）的强度成正相关。文章考察了这一原则对影响与信息的扩散（diffusion of influence and information）、流动机会（mobility opportunity）以及社区组织（community organization）所产生的作用，并着重强调 weak ties（弱关系）所具有的凝聚力量。大多数网络模型都在隐含地处理 strong ties（强关系），因而将其适用范围局限于边界清晰的小群体。而对弱关系的强调，则有助于讨论群体之间的关系，并有助于分析那些难以用首属群体（primary groups）来界定的社会结构片段。
Introduction · 引言
A fundamental weakness of current sociological theory is that it does not relate micro-level interactions to macro-level patterns in any convincing way. Large-scale statistical, as well as qualitative, studies offer a good deal of insight into such macro phenomena as social mobility, community organization, and political structure. At the micro level, a large and increasing body of data and theory offers useful and illuminating ideas about what transpires within the confines of the small group. But how interaction in small groups aggregates to form large-scale patterns eludes us in most cases.
当代社会学理论的一个根本缺陷在于，它未能以任何令人信服的方式把微观层次的互动与宏观层次的模式联系起来。大规模的统计研究以及定性研究，都为我们理解社会流动、社区组织和政治结构这类宏观现象提供了大量洞见。在微观层次上，规模庞大且与日俱增的资料与理论，也为我们理解小群体内部所发生的事情提供了有益而富有启发性的观念。然而，小群体中的互动究竟如何聚合成大规模的模式，在大多数情况下仍然是我们难以把握的问题。
I will argue, in this paper, that the analysis of processes in interpersonal networks provides the most fruitful micro-macro bridge. In one way or another, it is through these networks that small-scale interaction becomes translated into large-scale patterns, and that these, in turn, feed back into small groups.
我在本文中将要论证的是：对人际网络中各种过程的分析，提供了最富有成效的微观—宏观桥梁。无论以何种方式，正是通过这些网络，小规模互动才被转译为大规模的模式；而这些大规模模式又反过来回馈到小群体之中。
Sociometry, the precursor of network analysis, has always been curiously peripheral—invisible, really—in sociological theory. This is partly because it has usually been studied and applied only as a branch of social psychology; it is also because of the inherent complexities of precise network analysis. We have had neither the theory nor the measurement and sampling techniques to move sociometry from the usual small-group level to that of larger structures. While a number of stimulating and suggestive studies have recently moved in this direction (Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Milgram 1967; Boissevain 1968; Mitchell 1969), they do not treat structural issues in much theoretical detail. Studies which do so usually involve a level of technical complexity appropriate to such forbidding sources as the Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics, where the original motivation for the study of networks was that of developing a theory of neural, rather than social, interaction (see the useful review of this literature by Coleman [1960]; also Rapoport [1963]).
社会计量学（sociometry）作为网络分析的前身，在社会学理论中一直处于一种奇特的边缘地位——实际上可以说是隐形的。这一方面是因为它通常只被当作社会心理学的一个分支来研究和运用；另一方面则是因为精确的网络分析本身具有内在的复杂性。我们既缺乏相应的理论，也缺乏相应的测量与抽样技术，不足以把社会计量学从通常的小群体层次推进到更大结构的层次。尽管近来已有一些富有启发性和建设性的研究朝这一方向推进（Bott 1957；Mayer 1961；Milgram 1967；Boissevain 1968；Mitchell 1969），但它们并未在理论细节上充分处理结构问题。而那些确实处理了结构问题的研究，其技术复杂程度通常已经达到了《数学生物物理学公报》（Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics）这类令人望而生畏的文献所具有的水平；在这些文献中，研究网络的最初动机乃是要发展一种关于神经互动而非社会互动的理论（参见 Coleman [1960] 对这一文献的有益综述；另见 Rapoport [1963]）。
The strategy of the present paper is to choose a rather limited aspect of small-scale interaction—the strength of interpersonal ties—and to show, in some detail, how the use of network analysis can relate this aspect to such varied macro phenomena as diffusion, social mobility, political organization, and social cohesion in general. Section 1 · 第一节

THE STRENGTH OF TIES关系的强度

Most intuitive notions of the "strength" of an interpersonal tie should be satisfied by the following definition: the strength of a tie is a (probably linear) combination of the amount of time, the emotional intensity, the intimacy (mutual confiding), and the reciprocal services which characterize the tie.2 Ties discussed in this paper are assumed to be positive and symmetric; a comprehensive theory might require discussion of negative and/or asymmetric ties, but this would add unnecessary complexity to the present, exploratory comments.
3 Some anthropologists suggest "multiplexity," that is, multiple contents in a relationship, as indicating a strong tie (Kapferer 1969, p. 213). While this may be accurate in some circumstances, ties with only one content or with diffuse content may be strong as well (Simmel 1950, pp. 317–29). The present definition would show most multiplex ties to be strong but also allow for other possibilities.
4 Included in "absent" are both the lack of any relationship and ties without substantial significance, such as a "nodding" relationship between people living on the same street, or the "tie" to the vendor from whom one customarily buys a morning newspaper. That two people "know" each other by name need not move their relation out of this category if their interaction is negligible. In some contexts, however (disasters, for example), such "negligible" ties might usefully be distinguished from the absence of one. This is an ambiguity caused by substitution, for convenience of exposition, of discrete values for an underlying continuous variable.
对于人际关系的"强度"，大多数直觉性的理解都应当能为下述定义所满足：一个关系的强度，是（很可能是线性地）由刻画这一关系的四个要素组合而成——时间的多寡、情感的强度、亲密程度（相互倾诉）以及互惠性的服务。注2：本文所讨论的关系被假定为正向的、对称的；一种完备的理论或许需要讨论负向的和／或不对称的关系，但这会给本文探索性的论述平添不必要的复杂性。
注3：一些人类学家提出以"多重性"（multiplexity，即一段关系中包含多重内容）作为强关系的标志（Kapferer 1969, p. 213）。尽管这在某些情形下可能是对的，但只有单一内容或内容弥散的关系同样可能是强关系（Simmel 1950, pp. 317–29）。本文的定义会把大多数多重（multiplex）关系显示为强关系，但也为其他可能性留有余地。
注4：归入"缺失"（absent）这一类的，既包括根本没有任何关系，也包括缺乏实质意义的关系，例如住在同一条街上的人之间的"点头之交"，或者一个人与通常向其购买晨报的报贩之间的"关系"。如果两人之间的互动微不足道，那么他们"知道"彼此姓名这一点，并不必然使他们的关系脱离这一类别。然而在某些情境下（例如灾难），把这类"微不足道"的关系与"没有关系"区分开来可能是有益的。这是一种含混，其成因是为了叙述方便，用离散取值替代了 underlying continuous variable（底层连续变量）。
Each of these is somewhat independent of the other, though the set is obviously highly intracorrelated. Discussion of operational measures of and weights attaching to each of the four elements is postponed to future empirical studies. It is sufficient for the present purpose if most of us can agree, on a rough intuitive basis, whether a given tie is strong, weak, or absent.
这四个要素彼此之间在某种程度上相互独立，尽管这一组要素显然高度内部相关。关于这四个要素各自的操作化测量方法以及所附权重的讨论，我将推迟到今后的经验研究中再行处理。就本文的目的而言，只要我们大多数人能够在粗略的直觉基础上，就某一给定的关系是强的、弱的还是缺失的达成一致，便已足够。
Consider, now, any two arbitrarily selected individuals—call them A and B—and the set, S = C, D, E, ..., of all persons with ties to either or both of them. The hypothesis which enables us to relate dyadic ties to larger structures is: the stronger the tie between A and B, the larger the proportion of individuals in S to whom they will both be tied, that is, connected by a weak or strong tie. This overlap in their friendship circles is predicted to be least when their tie is absent, most when it is strong, and intermediate when it is weak.5 In Barnes's terminology, the union of their respective primary stars (1969, p. 58).｜注5：用巴恩斯（Barnes）的术语说，即两人各自 primary star（首要星形）的并集（1969, p. 58）。
现在，考虑任意选定的两个人——称他们为 A 和 B——以及集合 S = C, D, E, ...，即所有与其中一人或两人都有关联的人。使我们能够把二元关系与更大结构联系起来的假设是：A 与 B 之间的关系越强，他们共同与之关联（即以弱关系或强关系相连）的人在 S 中所占的比例就越大。据预测，他们友谊圈子的这种重叠，在二人关系缺失时最小，在关系强时最大，在关系弱时居中。注5：用巴恩斯（Barnes）的术语说，即两人各自 primary star（首要星形）的并集（1969, p. 58）。
The proposed relationship results, first, from the tendency (by definition) of stronger ties to involve larger time commitments. If A-B and A-C ties exist, then the amount of time C spends with B depends (in part) on the amount A spends with B and C, respectively. (If the events "A is with B" and "A is with C" were independent, then the event "C is with A and B" would have probability equal to the product of their probabilities. For example, if A and B are together 60% of the time, and A and C 40%, then C, A, and B would be together 24% of the time. Such independence would be less likely after than before B and C became acquainted.) If C and B have no relationship, common strong ties to A will probably bring them into interaction and generate one. Implicit here is Homans's idea that "the more frequently persons interact with one another, the stronger their sentiments of friendship for one another are apt to be" (1950, p. 133).
上述关系的形成，首先源于（按照定义）较强的关系往往牵涉更多的时间投入。如果 A-B 关系和 A-C 关系都存在，那么 C 与 B 相处的时间长短（部分地）取决于 A 分别与 B 和 C 相处的时间。（如果"A 与 B 在一起"和"A 与 C 在一起"这两个事件相互独立，那么"C 与 A、B 同时在一起"这一事件的概率就等于二者概率的乘积。例如，若 A 与 B 有 60% 的时间在一起，A 与 C 有 40% 的时间在一起，那么 C、A、B 三人同时在一起的时间就是 24%。相比 B 与 C 相识之前，这种独立性在他们相识之后出现的可能性更小。）如果 C 与 B 之间没有关系，那么他们与 A 共同的强关系很可能会把两人带入互动，并生成一段关系。这里隐含了霍曼斯（Homans）的观点："人们彼此互动越频繁，他们对彼此的友谊情感往往就越强烈"（1950, p. 133）。
The hypothesis is made plausible also by empirical evidence that the stronger the tie connecting two individuals, the more similar they are, in various ways (Berscheid and Walster 1969, pp. 69–91; Bramel 1969, pp. 9–16; Brown 1965, pp. 71–90; Laumann 1968; Newcomb 1961, chap. 5; Precker 1952). Thus, if strong ties connect A to B and A to C, both C and B, being similar to A, are probably similar to one another, increasing the likelihood of a friendship once they have met. Applied in reverse, these two factors—time and similarity—indicate why weaker A-B and A-C ties make a C-B tie less likely than strong ones: C and B are less likely to interact and less likely to be compatible if they do.
使这一假设显得合理的还有经验证据：连接两个人的关系越强，他们在各种方面就越相似（Berscheid and Walster 1969, pp. 69–91；Bramel 1969, pp. 9–16；Brown 1965, pp. 71–90；Laumann 1968；Newcomb 1961, 第5章；Precker 1952）。因此，如果强关系把 A 与 B、A 与 C 连接起来，那么 C 和 B 由于都与 A 相似，很可能彼此也相似，这就增加了二人一旦相识便结成友谊的可能性。反过来运用这两个因素——时间与相似性——便可说明，为什么较弱的 A-B 与 A-C 关系会使 C-B 关系出现的可能性低于强关系的情形：C 与 B 互动的可能性更小，而且即便互动，彼此相容的可能性也更小。
The theory of cognitive balance, as formulated by Heider (1958) and especially by Newcomb (1961, pp. 4–23), also predicts this result. If strong ties A-B and A-C exist, and if B and C are aware of one another, anything short of a positive tie would introduce a "psychological strain" into the situation since C will want his own feelings to be congruent with those of his good friend, A, and similarly, for B and his friend, A. Where the ties are weak, however, such consistency is psychologically less crucial. (On this point see also Homans [1950, p. 255] and Davis [1963, p. 448].)
由海德（Heider, 1958）提出、尤其由纽科姆（Newcomb, 1961, pp. 4–23）所阐发的认知平衡理论，也预测到了这一结果。如果 A-B 与 A-C 两个强关系存在，且 B 与 C 彼此知晓对方，那么任何不及正向关系的状态都会给这一情境引入"心理紧张"，因为 C 会希望自己的感受与其好友 A 的感受相一致；B 与其朋友 A 之间的关系亦是如此。然而，当关系较弱时，这种一致性在心理上就不那么关键了。（关于这一点，另见 Homans [1950, p. 255] 与 Davis [1963, p. 448]。）
Some direct evidence for the basic hypothesis exists (Kapferer 1969, p. 229 n.; Laumann and Schuman 1967; Rapoport and Horvath 1961; Rapoport 1963). This evidence is less comprehensive than one might hope. In addition, however, certain inferences from the hypothesis have received empirical support. Description of these inferences will suggest some of the substantive implications of the above argument.6 The models and experiments of Rapoport and his associates have been a major stimulus to this paper. Section 2 · 第二节

WEAK TIES IN DIFFUSION PROCESSES扩散过程中的弱关系

To derive implications for large networks of relations, it is necessary to frame the basic hypothesis more precisely. This can be done by investigating the possible triads consisting of strong, weak, or absent ties among A, B, and any arbitrarily chosen friend of either or both (i.e., some member of the set S, described above). A thorough mathematical model would do this in some detail, suggesting probabilities for various types. This analysis becomes rather involved, however, and it is sufficient for my purpose in this paper to say that the triad which is most unlikely to occur, under the hypothesis stated above, is that in which A and B are strongly linked, A has a strong tie to some friend C, but the tie between C and B is absent. This triad is shown in figure 1.
要推导出对大规模关系网络的意涵，有必要更精确地表述基本假设。这可以通过考察可能的三元组（triads）来实现：即在 A、B 以及任意选定的与其中一人或两人有朋友关系的人（即上文所述集合 S 中的某个成员）之间，由强关系、弱关系或缺失关系所构成的三元组。一个彻底的数学模型会较为详细地处理这一点，为各种类型给出概率。然而，这一分析会变得相当繁复；就本文的目的而言，只需说明以下一点便已足够：在上述假设之下，最不可能出现的三元组是——A 与 B 由强关系相连，A 与某位朋友 C 有强关系，而 C 与 B 之间的关系却缺失。这一三元组如图 1 所示。

To see the consequences of this assertion, I will exaggerate it in what follows by supposing that the triad shown never occurs—that is, that the B-C tie is always present (whether weak or strong), given the other two strong ties. Whatever results are inferred from this supposition should tend to occur in the degree that the triad in question tends to be absent.
为了看清这一论断的后果，我在下文中将把它加以夸大，假定图示的三元组永不出现——也就是说，在给定另外两条强关系的情况下，B-C 关系总是存在（无论其为弱为强）。凡是由这一假定推导出的结果，其发生的程度，应当与该三元组倾向于缺失的程度相对应。
Some evidence exists for this absence. Analyzing 651 sociograms, Davis (1970, p. 845) found that in 90% of them triads consisting of two mutual choices and one nonchoice occurred less than the expected random number of times. If we assume that mutual choice indicates a strong tie, this is strong evidence in the direction of my argument.7 This assumption is suggested by one of Davis's models (1970, p. 846) and made explicitly by Mazur (1971). It is not obvious, however. In a free-choice sociometric test or a fixed-choice one with a large number of choices, most strong ties would probably result in mutual choice, but some weak ones might as well. The significance of this triad's absence can be shown by using the concept of a "bridge"; this is a line in a network which provides the only path between two points (Harary, Norman, and Cartwright 1965, p. 198). Since, in general, each person has a great many contacts, a bridge between A and B provides the only route along which information or influence can flow from any contact of A to any contact of B, and, consequently, from anyone connected indirectly to A to anyone connected indirectly to B. Thus, in the study of diffusion, we can expect bridges to assume an important role.

Now, if the stipulated triad is absent, it follows that, except under unlikely conditions, no strong tie is a bridge. Since sociologists and anthropologists have carried out many hundreds of diffusion studies—Rogers's 1962 review dealt with 506—one might suppose that the above claims could easily be put to test. But this is not so, for several reasons. To begin with, though most diffusion studies find that personal contacts are crucial, many undertake no sociometric investigation. (Rogers [1962] discusses this point.) When sociometric techniques are used, they tend to discourage the naming of those weakly tied to the respondent by sharply limiting the numbers of choices allowed. Hence, the proposed importance of weak ties in diffusion is not measured. Even when more sociometric information is collected there is almost never an attempt to directly retrace the exact interpersonal paths traversed by an (idea, rumor, or) innovation. More commonly, the time when each individual adopted the innovation is recorded, as is the number of sociometric choices he received from others in the study. One point of controversy in diffusion studies can be related to my argument. Some have indicated that early innovators are marginal, that they "underconform to norms to such a degree that they are perceived as highly deviant" (Rogers 1962, p. 197). Others (e.g., Coleman, Katz, and Menzel [1966] on the adoption of a new drug by doctors) find that those named more frequently adopt an innovation substantially earlier. Becker (1970) tries to resolve the question of whether early innovators are "central" or "marginal" by referring to the "perceived risks of adoption of a given innovation." His study of public health innovations shows that when a new program is thought relatively safe and uncontroversial (as with the drug of Coleman et al.), central figures lead in its adoption; otherwise, marginal ones do (p. 273). He explains the difference in terms of a greater desire of "central" figures to protect their professional reputation.
扩散研究中的一处争议，可以与我的论证联系起来。一些研究者指出，早期的创新者是边缘人物，他们"对规范的遵从不足到了这样一种程度，以至于被视为高度偏离常规"（Rogers 1962, p. 197）。另一些研究者（例如 Coleman, Katz, and Menzel [1966] 关于医生采纳新药的研究）则发现，被提名次数更多的人，采纳创新的时间明显更早。贝克尔（Becker, 1970）试图借助"对采纳某一给定创新所感知到的风险"来解决早期创新者究竟是"中心的"还是"边缘的"这一问题。他对公共卫生创新的研究表明：当一项新计划被认为相对安全且无争议时（如 Coleman 等人所研究的那种药物），中心人物在采纳上领先；否则，领先的就是边缘人物（p. 273）。他用"中心"人物更强烈地希望维护自己职业声誉这一点来解释这种差异。
Kerckhoff, Back, and Miller (1965) reach a similar conclusion in a different type of study. A Southern textile plant had been swept by "hysterical contagion": a few, then more and more workers, claiming bites from a mysterious "insect," became nauseous, numb, and weak, leading to a plant shutdown. When the affected workers were asked to name their three best friends, many named one another, but the very earliest to be stricken were social isolates, receiving almost no choices. An explanation, compatible with Becker's, is offered: since the symptoms might be thought odd, early "adopters" were likely to be found among the marginal, those less subject to social pressures. Later, "it is increasingly likely that some persons who are socially integrated will be affected. . . . The contagion enters social networks and is disseminated with increasing rapidity" (p. 13). This is consistent with Rogers's comment that while the first adopters of innovations are marginal, the next group, "early adopters," "are a more integrated part of the local social system than the innovators" (1962, p. 183).
柯克霍夫、巴克与米勒（Kerckhoff, Back, and Miller 1965）在一项不同类型的研究中得出了相似的结论。南方一家纺织厂曾爆发"癔症性传染"（hysterical contagion）：先是少数、随后越来越多的工人声称被一种神秘的"昆虫"咬伤，出现恶心、麻木和虚弱症状，最终导致工厂停工。当要求受影响的工人说出他们最好的三位朋友时，许多人彼此提名；但最早发病的那些人却是社会孤立者，几乎没有人选择他们。作者给出了一个与贝克尔相容的解释：由于这些症状可能被视为怪异，早期的"采纳者"很可能出现在边缘人物之中，也就是那些较少受到社会压力影响的人。到后来，"一些社会融入程度较高的人受到影响的可能性越来越大……这种传染进入社会网络，并以越来越快的速度传播开来"（p. 13）。这与罗杰斯的评论相一致：虽然创新的首批采纳者是边缘人物，但紧接着的"早期采纳者"群体"比创新者更深度地融入了当地社会系统"（1962, p. 183）。
"Central" and "marginal" individuals may well be motivated as claimed; but if the marginal are genuinely so, it is difficult to see how they can ever spread innovations successfully. A somewhat different kind of diffusion study offers more direct support: the "small-world" investigations of Milgram and his associates. In this section and the next, I want to discuss the general significance of the above findings and arguments at two levels: first that of individuals, then that of communities. These discussions make no pretense of being comprehensive; they are meant only to illustrate possible applications.

In recent years, a great deal of literature has appeared analyzing the impact on the behavior of individuals of the social networks in which they are imbedded. Some of the studies have emphasized the ways in which behavior is shaped and constrained by one's network (Bott 1957; Mayer 1961; Frankenberg 1965), others the ways in which individuals can manipulate these networks to achieve specific goals (Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968; Kapferer 1969). Both facets are generally supposed to be affected by the structure of one's network. Bott argued that the crucial variable is that of whether one's friends tend to know one another ("close-knit" network) or not ("loose-knit" network). Barnes makes this dichotomy into a continuous variable by counting the number of ties observed in the network formed by ego and his friends and dividing it by the ratio of possible ones; this then corresponds to what is often called network "density" (Barnes 1969; Tilly 1969).14 But if the crucial question is really whether ego's friends know each other, this measure should probably be computed after ego and his ties have been subtracted from the network; distortions caused by failure to do so will be especially great in small networks. It is important to note, also, that in mon egocentric networks, there is no simple correspondence between density and any "average" measure of the extent to which the various egos have friends who know one another. Epstein (1969) points out, however, that different parts of ego's network may have different density. He calls those with whom one "interacts most intensely and most regularly, and who are therefore also likely to come to know one another," the "effective network"; the "remainder constitute the extended network" (pp. 110–11). This is close to saying, in my terms, that one's strong ties form a dense network, one's weak ties a less dense one. I would add that one's weak ties which are not local bridges might as well be counted with the strong ties, to maximize separation of the dense from the less dense network sectors.
然而，爱泼斯坦（Epstein, 1969）指出，自我网络的不同部分可能具有不同的密度。他把那些"与之互动最为强烈、最为规律，因而彼此也最可能相识"的人称为"有效网络"（effective network）；"其余的人构成扩展网络"（extended network）（pp. 110–11）。用我的术语来说，这几乎就等于说：一个人的强关系构成一个高密度的网络，其弱关系构成一个密度较低的网络。我还要补充一点：一个人的弱关系中那些并非局部桥的部分，不妨与强关系归为一类，以便最大程度地把高密度的网络部门与低密度的网络部门区分开来。
One point on which there is no general agreement is whether ego's network should be treated as composed only of those to whom he is tied directly, or should include the contacts of his contacts, and/or others. Analyses stressing encapsulation of an individual by his network tend to take the former position, those stressing manipulation of networks, the latter, since information or favors available through direct contacts may depend on who their contacts are. I would argue that by dividing ego's network into that part made up of strong and nonbridging weak ties on the one hand, and that of bridging weak ties on the other, both orientations can be dealt with. Ties in the former part should tend to be to people who not only know one another, but who also have few contacts not tied to ego as well. Such a model of job-information flow actually does correspond to the economists' model of a "perfect" labor market. The usual dichotomy between "formal" or mass procedures and diffusion through personal contacts may thus be invalid in some cases where, instead, the former may be seen as a limiting case of long diffusion chains. From the individual's point of view, then, weak ties are an important resource in making possible mobility opportunity. Seen from a more macroscopic vantage, weak ties play a role in effecting social cohesion. When a man changes jobs, he is not only moving from one network of ties to another, but also establishing a link between these. Such a link is often of the same kind which facilitated his own movement. Especially within professional and technical specialties which are well defined and limited in size, this mobility sets up elaborate structures of bridging weak ties between the more coherent clusters that constitute operative networks in particular locations. Information and ideas thus flow more easily through the specialty, giving it some "sense of community," activated at meetings and conventions. Maintenance of weak ties may well be the most important consequence of such meetings.
因此，从个体的角度来看，弱关系是使流动机会成为可能的一项重要资源。而从更为宏观的视角来看，弱关系在实现社会凝聚力方面扮演着角色。当一个人更换工作时，他不仅是从一个关系网络移动到另一个关系网络，同时也是在这两个网络之间建立起一条连接。这样一条连接，往往与促成他自己流动的那类连接属于同一种。尤其是在那些边界清晰、规模有限的专业与技术门类内部，这种流动会在特定的地点上构成运作网络的、较为连贯的簇（clusters）之间，建立起由具架桥作用的弱关系构成的精细结构。信息与观念由此得以更顺畅地在这一专业门类内部流动，赋予它某种"共同体感"（sense of community），并在各种会议与大会上被激活。维系弱关系，很可能正是这类会议最重要的结果。

WEAK TIES AND COMMUNITY ORGANIZATION弱关系与社区组织

These comments about sense of community may remind us that in many cases it is desirable to deal with a unit of analysis larger than a single individual. I would like to develop my argument further by analyzing, in this section, why some communities organize for common goals easily and effectively whereas others seem unable to mobilize resources, even against dire threats. The Italian community of Boston's West End, for example, was unable to even form an organization to fight against the "urban renewal" which ultimately destroyed it. This seems especially anomalous in view of Gans's description of West End social structure as cohesive (1962).
上述关于共同体感的评论或许会提醒我们：在许多情况下，有必要处理一个大于单一个体的分析单位。在本节中，我想通过分析这样一个问题来进一步展开我的论证：为什么有些社区能够轻易而有效地为共同目标组织起来，而另一些社区却似乎无力动员资源，即便面对可怕的威胁也是如此。例如，波士顿西区（West End）的意大利裔社区，甚至未能组建起一个组织来对抗最终毁灭了它的"城市更新"（urban renewal）。鉴于甘斯（Gans, 1962）把西区的社会结构描述为具有凝聚力的，这一点就显得尤为反常。
Variations in culture and personality are often cited to explain such anomalies. Gans contrasts "lower"-, "working"-, and "middle"-class subcultures, concluding that only the last provides sufficient trust in leaders and practice in working toward common goals to enable formation of an effective organization. Thus, the working-class West End could not resist urban renewal (pp. 229–304). Yet, numerous well-documented cases show that some working-class communities have mobilized quite successfully against comparable or lesser threats (Dahl 1961, pp. 192–99; Keyes 1969; Davies 1966, chap. 4).19 This point was brought to my attention by Richard Wolfe.｜注19：这一点由理查德·沃尔夫（Richard Wolfe）提醒我注意。 I would suggest, as a sharper analytical tool, examination of the network of ties comprising a community to see whether aspects of its structure might facilitate or block organization.
文化与人格上的差异，常被援引来解释这类反常现象。甘斯对比了"下层"、"工人"与"中产"阶级的亚文化，得出结论说：只有后者才提供了对领导者充分的信任、以及为共同目标而工作的充分实践，从而使有效组织的形成成为可能。因此，工人阶级的西区无力抵抗城市更新（pp. 229–304）。然而，大量有据可查的案例表明，某些工人阶级社区曾针对相当或更小的威胁进行了相当成功的动员（Dahl 1961, pp. 192–99; Keyes 1969; Davies 1966, 第4章）。注19：这一点由理查德·沃尔夫（Richard Wolfe）提醒我注意。我要提出的是一个更为锐利的分析工具：考察构成一个社区的关系网络，看看其结构的各个方面究竟是促进还是阻碍了组织。
Imagine, to begin with, a community completely partitioned into cliques, such that each person is tied to every other in his clique and to none outside. Community organization would be severely inhibited. Leafletting, radio announcements, or other methods could insure that everyone was aware of some nascent organization; but studies of diffusion and mass communication have shown that people rarely act on mass-media information unless it is also transmitted through personal ties (Katz and Lazarsfeld 1955; Rogers 1962); otherwise one has no particular reason to think that an advertised product or an organization should be taken seriously. Enthusiasm for an organization in one clique, then, would not spread to others but would have to develop independently in each one to insure success.
首先，设想一个被完全分割为若干派系的社区：每个人都与本派系内的所有人相连，而与派系外的任何人都不相连。这样的社区组织将受到严重抑制。散发传单、广播公告或其他方法，可以确保每个人都知晓某个新生组织的存在；但关于扩散与大众传播的研究已经表明，除非信息同时也经由个人关系传递，人们很少会根据大众媒介的信息采取行动（Katz and Lazarsfeld 1955; Rogers 1962）；否则，人们并没有特别的理由认为一个被宣传的产品或组织应当被认真对待。这样一来，某一派系中对某个组织的热情就不会扩散到其他派系，而必须在每一个派系中独立地生发出来，才能保证成功。
The problem of trust is closely related. I would propose that whether a person trusts a given leader depends heavily on whether there exist intermediary personal contacts who can, from their own knowledge, assure him that the leader is trustworthy, and who can, if necessary, intercede with the leader or his lieutenants on his behalf. Trust in leaders is integrally related to the capacity to predict and affect their behavior. Leaders, for their part, have little motivation to be responsive or even trustworthy toward those to whom they have no direct or indirect connection. Thus, network fragmentation, by reducing drastically the number of paths from any leader to his potential followers, would inhibit trust in such leaders. This inhibition, furthermore, would not be entirely irrational.
信任问题与之密切相关。我要提出的是：一个人是否信任某位特定的领导者，在很大程度上取决于是否存在这样一些中间性的个人接触——他们能够根据自己的了解，向他保证这位领导者值得信任，并且在必要时能够代表他向这位领导者或其副手说情。对领导者的信任，与预测并影响其行为的能力是内在相关的。就领导者一方而言，对于那些与自己没有任何直接或间接联系的人，他们几乎没有什么动机去作出回应，甚至没有什么动机去保持可信。因此，网络的碎片化（fragmentation）由于急剧减少了从任何一位领导者到其潜在追随者之间的路径数目，将会抑制人们对这类领导者的信任。而且，这种抑制并非完全非理性的。
Could the West End's social structure really have been of this kind? Note first that while the structure hypothesized is, by definition, extremely fragmented, this is evident only at a macroscopic level—from an "aerial view" of the network. The local phenomenon is cohesion. (Davis [1967] also noted this paradox, in a related context.) An analyst studying such a group by participant observation might never see the extent of fragmentation, especially if the cliques were not earmarked by ethnic, cultural, or other visible differences. In the nature of participant observation, one is likely to get caught up in a fairly restricted circle; a few useful contacts are acquired and relied on for introduction to others. The "problem of entry into West End society was particularly vexing," Gans writes. But eventually, he and his wife "were welcomed by one of our neighbors and became friends with them. As a result they invited us to many of their evening gatherings and introduced us to other neighbors, relatives and friends. ... As time went on ... other West Enders ... introduced me to relatives and friends, although most of the social gatherings at which I participated were those of our first contact and their circle" (1962, pp. 340–41; emphasis supplied). Thus, his account of cohesive groups is not inconsistent with overall fragmentation.

Now, suppose that all ties in the West End were either strong or absent, and that the triad of figure 1 did not occur. Then, for any ego, all his friends were friends of one another, and all their friends were ego's friends as well. Unless each person was strongly tied to all others in the community, network structure did indeed break down into the isolated cliques posited above. (In terms of Davis's mathematical treatment, the overall network was "clusterable," with unique clusters [1967, p. 186].) Since it is unlikely that anyone could sustain more than a few dozen strong ties, this would, in fact, have been the result.
现在，假设西区中的所有关系要么强要么缺失，且图 1 的三元组不出现。那么，对任何一个自我而言，他所有的朋友彼此都是朋友，而这些朋友的所有朋友也都是自我的朋友。除非每个人都与社区中的所有其他人有强关系，否则网络结构确实就会瓦解为上文所假定的那些孤立的派系。（用戴维斯的数学处理来说，整个网络是"可聚类的"（clusterable），并具有唯一的簇 [1967, p. 186]。）由于任何人都不大可能维持超过几十条的强关系，这事实上就会成为结果。
Did strong ties take up enough of the West Enders' social time to make this analysis even approximately applicable? Gans reported that "sociability is a routinized gathering of a relatively unchanging peer group of family members and friends that takes place several times a week." Some "participate in informal cliques and in clubs made up of unrelated people. ... In number, and in the amount of time devoted to them, however, these groups are much less important than the family circle" (1962, pp. 74, 80). Moreover, two common sources of weak ties, formal organizations and work settings, did not provide them for the West End; organization membership was almost nil (pp. 104–7) and few worked within the area itself, so that ties formed at work were not relevant to the community (p. 122).
强关系是否占据了西区居民社交时间的足够份额，从而使这一分析至少近似地适用？甘斯报告说："社交活动是一种常规化的聚会，参与者是相对固定的、由家庭成员和朋友构成的同辈群体，每周聚会数次。"有些人"参与由无亲属关系的人组成的非正式派系与俱乐部……然而，无论在数量上，还是在投入于其中的时间上，这些群体都远不如家庭圈子重要"（1962, pp. 74, 80）。此外，弱关系的两个常见来源——正式组织与工作场所——在西区也未能提供弱关系：组织成员身份几乎为零（pp. 104–7），而且很少有人在该地区内部工作，因此在工作中形成的关系与社区无关（p. 122）。

The case of Charlestown, a working-class community which successfully organized against the urban renewal plan of the same city (Boston) against which the West End was powerless, is instructive in this respect: unlike the West End, it had a rich organizational life, and most male residents worked within the area (Keyes 1969, chap. 4).
查尔斯顿（Charlestown）的案例在这方面颇有启发：这是一个工人阶级社区，它成功地组织起来，抵制了同一个城市（波士顿）的城市更新计划——而面对同一计划，西区却无能为力。与西区不同，查尔斯顿拥有丰富的组织生活，而且大多数男性居民都在该地区内部工作（Keyes 1969, 第4章）。
In the absence of actual network data, all this is speculation. The hard information needed to show either that the West End was fragmented or that communities which organized successfully were not, and that both patterns were due to the strategic role of weak ties, is not at hand and would not have been simple to collect. Nor has comparable information been collected in any context. But a theoretical framework has, at least, been suggested, with which one could not only carry out analyses post hoc, but also predict differential capacity of communities to act toward common goals. A rough principle with which to begin such an investigation might be: the more local bridges (per person?) in a community and the greater their degree, the more cohesive the community and the more capable of acting in concert. Study of the origins and nature (strength and content, for example) of such bridging ties would then offer unusual insight into the social dynamics of the community.
在缺乏实际网络数据的情况下，所有这些都只是推测。要证明西区是碎片化的，或者证明那些成功组织起来的社区并非如此，并且证明这两种模式都源于弱关系的战略性作用，所需要的硬信息目前并不具备，而且收集起来也并不简单。在任何其他情境中，也未曾收集过可比的信息。但至少，我们已经提出了一个理论框架；借助它，人们不仅可以进行事后分析，还可以预测不同社区为共同目标而行动的能力差异。用以开启这样一项研究的一条粗略原则或许是：一个社区中的局部桥（人均？）越多、其度数越高，该社区就越有凝聚力，也就越有能力采取一致的行动。进而，对这类架桥性关系的起源与性质（例如其强度与内容）的研究，将为理解社区的社会动力学提供非同寻常的洞见。
Section 5 · 第五节

MICRO AND MACRO NETWORK MODELS微观与宏观网络模型

Unlike most models of interpersonal networks, the one presented here is not meant primarily for application to small, face-to-face groups or to groups in confined institutional or organizational settings. Rather, it is meant for linkage of such small-scale levels with one another and with larger, more amorphous ones. This is why emphasis here has been placed more on weak ties than on strong. Weak ties are more likely to link members of different small groups than are strong ones, which tend to be concentrated within particular groups.
与大多数人际网络模型不同，本文所提出的模型，其主要用途并不在于应用于面对面交往的小群体，或处于封闭的制度性、组织性设置中的群体。相反，它的用途在于把这类小规模层次彼此连接起来，并把它们与更大、更无定形的层次连接起来。这正是本文把重点更多地放在弱关系而非强关系上的原因。与强关系相比，弱关系更有可能把不同小群体的成员连接起来；而强关系则往往集中在特定的群体内部。
For this reason, my discussion does not lend itself to elucidation of the internal structure of small groups. This point can be made more clearly by contrasting the model of this paper to one with which it shares many similarities, that of James Davis, Paul Holland, and Samuel Leinhardt (hereafter, the DHL model) (Davis 1970; Davis and Leinhardt 1971; Holland and Leinhardt 1970, 1971a, 1971b; Davis, Holland, and Leinhardt 1971; Leinhardt 1972). The authors, inspired by certain propositions in George Homans's The Human Group (1950), argue that "the central proposition in structural sociometry is this: Interpersonal choices tend to be transitive—if P chooses O and O chooses X, then P is likely to choose X" (Davis et al. 1971, p. 309). When this is true without exception, a sociogram can be divided into cliques in which every individual chooses every other; any asymmetric choices or nonchoices are between such cliques, and asymmetry, if present, runs only in one direction. A partial ordering of cliques may thus be inferred. If mutual choice implies equal, and asymmetric choice unequal, status, then this ordering reflects the stratification structure of the group (Holland and Leinhardt 1971a, pp. 107–14).
由于这一原因，我的讨论并不适用于阐明小群体的内部结构。把本文的模型与另一个与之有诸多相似之处的模型——詹姆斯·戴维斯、保罗·霍兰德与塞缪尔·莱因哈特的模型（下文简称 DHL 模型）——加以对照，可以把这一点说得更清楚（Davis 1970; Davis and Leinhardt 1971; Holland and Leinhardt 1970, 1971a, 1971b; Davis, Holland, and Leinhardt 1971; Leinhardt 1972）。这些作者受乔治·霍曼斯《人类群体》（The Human Group, 1950）中某些命题的启发，主张"结构社会计量学的中心命题是这样：人际选择倾向于具有传递性——如果 P 选择 O，O 选择 X，那么 P 很可能选择 X"（Davis et al. 1971, p. 309）。当这一点毫无例外地成立时，一张社会关系图就可以被划分为若干派系，其中每个人都选择其他每个人；任何不对称的选择或未作的选择都发生在这类派系之间，而且不对称（如果存在的话）只沿一个方向运行。由此可以推断出派系之间的一个偏序（partial ordering）。如果互相选择意味着地位相等、而不对称选择意味着地位不等，那么这一排序就反映了群体的分层结构（Holland and Leinhardt 1971a, pp. 107–14）。
One immediate difference between this model and mine is that it is cast in terms of "choices" rather than ties. Most sociometric tests ask people whom they like best or would prefer to do something with, rather than with whom they actually spend time. If transitivity is built more into our cognitive than our social structure, this method might overstate its prevalence. But since the DHL model could recast in terms of ties, this is not a conclusive difference.
这一模型与我的模型之间一个直接的区别在于：它是用"选择"而非关系来表述的。大多数社会计量测验询问的是人们最喜欢谁、或最愿意与谁一起做某事，而不是他们实际与谁共度时光。如果传递性更多地被建构在我们的认知结构之中，而非社会结构之中，那么这一方法就可能高估传递性的普遍程度。不过，由于 DHL 模型可以用关系来重新表述，这并不是一个决定性的区别。
More significant is the difference in the application of my argument to transitivity. Let P choose O and O choose X (or equivalently, let X choose O and O choose P): then I assert that transitivity—P choosing X (or X, P)—is most likely when both ties—P-O and O-X—are strong, least likely when both are weak, and of intermediate probability if one is strong and one weak. Transitivity, then, is claimed to be a function of the strength of ties, rather than a general feature of social structure.
更为重要的区别，在于我的论证在传递性问题上的应用方式。令 P 选择 O、O 选择 X（或者等价地，令 X 选择 O、O 选择 P）：那么我要断言的是，传递性——即 P 选择 X（或 X 选择 P）——在 P-O 与 O-X 两条关系都为强时最可能出现，在二者都为弱时最不可能出现，而在一强一弱时具有中等概率。由此可见，传递性被主张为关系强度的函数，而非社会结构的一般特征。
The justification of this assertion is, in part, identical with that offered earlier for the triad designated A-B-C. In addition, it is important to point out here that the DHL model was designed for small groups, and with increasing size of the group considered the rationale for transitivity weakens. If P chooses O and O chooses X, P should choose X out of consistency; but if P does not know or barely knows X, nonchoice implies no inconsistency. For the logic of transitivity to apply, a group must be small enough so that any person knows enough about every other person to be able to decide whether to "choose" him, and encounters him often enough that he feels the need for such a decision. Including weak ties in my model, then, lessens the expectation of transitivity and permits analysis of intergroup relationships and also of amorphous chunks of social structure which an analyst may ferret out as being of interest, but which are not easily defined in terms of face-to-face groups. Anthropologists have recently referred to such chunks as "quasi-groups" (Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968).
对这一论断的辩护，有一部分与前文就 A-B-C 三元组所作的辩护相同。此外，这里需要指出的重要一点是：DHL 模型是为小群体设计的，而随着所考察群体规模的增大，传递性的理据就会减弱。如果 P 选择 O、O 选择 X，那么出于一致性，P 应当选择 X；但如果 P 不认识 X 或几乎不认识 X，那么不作选择并不蕴含任何不一致。要让传递性的逻辑成立，一个群体必须小到这样的程度：任何人对其他每一个人都了解得足够多，足以决定是否"选择"他；并且与他碰面的频率足够高，以至于感到有必要作出这一决定。因此，在我的模型中纳入弱关系，会降低对传递性的预期，并使得分析群体间关系成为可能，也使得分析那些分析者可能挖掘出来、认为值得关注、却难以用面对面群体来界定的无定形的社会结构块成为可能。人类学家近来把这类结构块称为"准群体"（quasi-groups）（Mayer 1966; Boissevain 1968）。
Since, as I have argued above, weak ties are poorly represented in sociograms, there is little in the DHL empirical studies—which apply statistical tests to sociometric data—to confirm or disconfirm my argument on transitivity. One finding does lend itself to speculation, however. Leinhardt (1972) shows that the sociograms of schoolchildren conform more and more closely to the transitive model as they become older, sixth graders being the oldest tested. He interprets this as reflecting cognitive development—increasing capacity to make use of transitive logic. If my assertion is correct, an alternative possibility would be that children develop stronger ties with increasing age. This is consistent with some theories of child development (see especially Sullivan 1953, chap. 16) and would imply, on my argument, greater transitivity of structure. Some support for this explanation comes from Leinhardt's finding that proportion of choices which were mutual was positively correlated with both grade level and degree of transitivity. In these sociograms, with an average of only about four choices per child, it seems likely that most mutual choices reflected strong ties (see n. 7, above).
由于（正如我上文所论证的）弱关系在社会关系图中表征得很不充分，在 DHL 的那些经验研究——它们把统计检验应用于社会计量数据——之中，几乎没有多少东西能够证实或证伪我关于传递性的论证。不过，有一项发现确实引人遐想。莱因哈特（Leinhardt, 1972）表明，随着学童年龄的增长（其所测试的最高年级为六年级），他们的社会关系图越来越贴合传递性模型。他把这一点解释为认知发展的反映——即运用传递性逻辑的能力在增强。如果我的论断是正确的，另一种可能性就是：儿童随着年龄增长而发展出更强的关系。这与某些儿童发展理论相一致（尤其见 Sullivan 1953, 第16章），并且按照我的论证，将意味着结构具有更强的传递性。为这一解释提供若干支持的是莱因哈特的另一发现：互选所占的比例，与年级水平以及传递性程度都呈正相关。在这些社会关系图中，每个儿童平均只有约四个选择，因此大多数互选很可能反映的正是强关系（见上文注 7）。
Section 6 · 第六节

CONCLUSION结论

The major implication intended by this paper is that the personal experience of individuals is closely bound up with larger-scale aspects of social structure, well beyond the purview or control of particular individuals. Linkage of micro and macro levels is thus no luxury but of central importance to the development of sociological theory. Such linkage generates paradoxes: weak ties, often denounced as generative of alienation (Wirth 1938) are here seen as indispensable to individuals' opportunities and to their integration into communities; strong ties, breeding local cohesion, lead to overall fragmentation. Paradoxes are a welcome antidote to theories which explain everything all too neatly.
本文想要传达的主要意涵是：个体的个人经验，与社会结构中那些远远超出特定个体的视野或控制范围的更大规模的方面，紧密地纠缠在一起。因此，微观层次与宏观层次的连接绝非可有可无的奢侈品，而是社会学理论发展的核心要务。这种连接会产生出悖论：弱关系——常被斥为产生疏离（alienation）的根源（Wirth 1938）——在这里却被看作是个体的机会、以及个体融入社区所不可或缺的；而强关系在培育局部凝聚力的同时，却导致了整体的碎片化。对于那种把一切都解释得过于井井有条的理论而言，悖论是一剂受欢迎的解药。
The model offered here is a very limited step in the linking of levels; it is a fragment of a theory. Treating only the strength of ties ignores, for instance, all the important issues involving their content. What is the relation between strength and degree of specialization of ties, or between strength and hierarchical structure? How can "negative" ties be handled? Should tie strength be developed as a continuous variable? What is the developmental sequence of network structure over time?
本文所提供的模型，在连接各个层次的努力中只是非常有限的一步；它只是理论的一个片段。例如，仅仅处理关系的强度，就忽略了所有涉及关系内容的重要议题。关系的强度与其专门化程度之间是什么关系？强度与等级结构之间又是什么关系？"负向"关系应当如何处理？关系强度是否应当被发展为一个连续变量？网络结构随时间的演化序列又是怎样的？
As such questions are resolved, others will arise. Demography, coalition structure, and mobility are just a few of the variables which would be of special importance in developing micro-macro linkage with the help of network analysis; how these are related to the present discussion needs specification. My contribution here is mainly, then, exploratory and programmatic, its primary purpose being to generate interest in the proposed program of theory and research.
随着这些问题得到解决，新的问题又会出现。人口构成、联盟结构与流动，只是借助网络分析来发展微观—宏观连接时具有特殊重要性的变量中的少数几个；它们与本文讨论之间的关系，需要加以明确说明。因此，我在这里的贡献主要是探索性的与纲领性的，其首要目的在于引起人们对所提出的理论与研究的纲领的兴趣。
